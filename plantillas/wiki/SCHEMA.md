# Wiki Schema — {{CANAL}}

## Domain
Memoria del canal de YouTube {{CANAL}} (hermes-youtube): quién presenta y a quién habla, su voz, sus pilares y
formatos, las decisiones y su porqué, lo que la persona pidió corregir y las lecciones. **No** es un almacén de
material: las fuentes viven en `fuentes/`, las piezas en `inventario.json` y los guiones en los encargos; aquí solo
se resumen y enlazan.

## Conventions
- File names: lowercase, hyphens, no spaces (e.g., `banco-analogias.md`). Pages in the channel's language.
- Every page starts with YAML frontmatter (see below). Use `[[wikilinks]]` (at least 2 outbound links per page).
- **Enlazar, no copiar**: cite encargos (`encargo guion-tema-x-1a2b`), puertas (`puerta g…`) and sources with their
  minute; never paste whole scripts or transcripts here.
- When updating a page, bump `updated`. Every new page goes into `index.md`; every action is appended to `log.md`.

## Frontmatter
```yaml
---
title: Page Title
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
type: entity | concept | comparison | query | summary
tags: [from taxonomy below]
sources: [fuentes/…, encargo …, puerta …, conversación YYYY-MM-DD]
confidence: high | medium | low
contested: true            # optional
---
```

## Tag Taxonomy
- Canal: canal, audiencia, pilar, formato, marca
- Voz: voz, muletilla, analogia, ejemplo
- Contenido: tema, titulo, miniatura, guion, dato, exclusion
- Proceso: decision, correccion, leccion, riesgo
- Meta: comparacion, pregunta-abierta

Rule: every tag on a page must appear here. Add a new tag here first, then use it.

## Page Thresholds
- **Create a page** for a decision with consequences, a recurring trait of the presenter's voice, a format that
  works, a risk, or a comparison the person asked for.
- **Don't create a page** for passing remarks or for material that already lives in `fuentes/` or in an encargo.
- **Archive** (`_archive/`) when superseded; never delete.

## Fixed pages (written by hermes-youtube, no model)
`concepts/canal.md`, `decisiones.md` and `correcciones.md` are written and regenerated by the system at fixed
moments (configuration changes and every gate decision). `concepts/voz.md` is written when a person approves the
voice profile. Don't edit them by hand: write your own pages next to them and link them. Every stage receives the
fixed pages that concern it in its context.
